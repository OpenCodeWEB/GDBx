How it works

  1. Mine PoW over name:pub:target:ts:nonce (diff 4/3/2)
  2. Sign claim {name,target,ownerPub,ts} with GDBx envelope
  3. Store at tld/gdbx/<name> via POST /sync
  4. Resolve GET /name/:name re-verifies PoW + sig
  5. Short link /n/:name 302-redirects if http(s)
